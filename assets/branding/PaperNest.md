# PaperNest logo

The final icon combines open ivory paper pages, a green nest and a fountain pen.
Created with the built-in image_gen tool; approved base artwork was edited to add the pen.
App asset: `static/papernest-icon.png`. Windows sizes 16–256 px are generated by
`python assets/branding/build_windows_icon.py` into `static/papernest.ico`.

## Final edit prompt

Edit the provided PaperNest app logo. Preserve the approved open ivory paper pages, interwoven forest-green and sage nest, composition, softly dimensional style, and genuine transparent background. Add exactly one elegant clearly recognizable writing pen diagonally across the right-hand ivory page, tip toward the lower center and upper end toward upper right. A forest-green fountain pen with a restrained warm brass nib, strong simple silhouette, sized clearly enough to identify in a small Windows icon without dominating or concealing the open-book-and-nest emblem. Match existing lighting and materials. Change only what is necessary to integrate the pen. No text, letters, watermark, background rectangle, other objects, or surrounding scene. Transparent production-ready square app icon.
